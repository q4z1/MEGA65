# Smaragd Dash's help pages (tools/mkhelp.py makes SMARAGD.HELP of them;
# Help or H on the title shows them, a key turns the page).
#
#   = TITLE                 a new page (up to 20 characters)
#   - text                  a line of text (up to 38 characters, from the
#                           screen's second column; more spaces: further in)
#   -                       an empty line
#   ELEMENT  NAME  WHAT     GDash's element (O_..., its tile as in the
#                           editor), its name (up to 10 characters) and what
#                           it does (up to 22), two or more spaces apart
#
# A page has 10 lines; more go on to a page of the same title. Only the
# font's characters: letters, digits, space and / : . ! - , + * < > ^ _
# What the elements do is GDash's engine (caverenderedengine.cpp).

= HOW TO PLAY
- DIG FOR EMERALDS: ENOUGH OF THEM OPEN
- THE EXIT. GET THERE BEFORE TIME IS UP.
-
-  MOVE      JOYSTICK OR CURSOR KEYS
-  SNAP      FIRE AND A DIRECTION
-  P         PAUSE
-  RUN/STOP  GIVE UP THE LIFE
-  F1        END THE GAME
-  I         HELD: WHAT ROCKFORD CARRIES
-  TITLE     E EDITOR  RUN/STOP BASIC
